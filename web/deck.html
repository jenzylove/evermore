Evermore is a game that cannot be switched off. Your hero is a CashToken in your wallet, and the rules that level it up are Bitcoin Cash contracts. Built solo by JDollar.

02 · How it plays

Raise. Fight. Forge. Earn while you sleep.

Raise

Mint a Knight, Rogue or Mage. Stats live inside the token.

Challenge

Fight echoes of other players' heroes. The fee goes to their keeper.

Duel

Strike, Guard or Special. Quick turn based fights, in 3D.

Forge

The chain replays your win, then levels your hero. Beat stronger echoes for capped gear.

Earn away

Your hero is an echo too. Every challenge against it pays you.

Trade

Sell any hero at your price. Hero and money swap in one transaction.

03 · Main benefits

Keepers are paid on every challenge, inside the same transaction. No payout queue.

It feels like a game

3D fighters, sound, one tap front door, free practice. A play Satchel means no wallet popups mid fight.
