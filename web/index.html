02 · How it plays

Raise a hero. Fight the echoes. Keep it forever.

I

Raise

Choose a Knight, a Rogue or a Mage. Your hero is minted as a token you own, with its level and stats written inside it.

II

Challenge

Every opponent is the echo of a real player's hero. Pick one you can beat. Your challenge pays 2 cents to its keeper.

III

Duel

Strike, Guard or Special, round by round. Read the echo's style, charge your special, land the last blow.

IV

Forge

Win and Bitcoin Cash replays the fight before it seals your new level. Armour, glow, a halo, a crown: your hero changes as it grows.

V

Earn while away

Your own hero stands in the arena as an echo. Every player who challenges it pays you, even while you sleep.

VI

Trade

A levelled hero is worth real money. Sell it to anyone, hero and payment swap in one transaction.

06 · Why Bitcoin Cash

The chain is the referee.

Every win is checked

A Bitcoin Cash contract replays your moves before your hero can level up. A fake win is rejected by the network itself.

Your hero is yours

Heroes and gear are CashTokens in your own wallet. No company can delete, freeze or confiscate them.

Keepers are paid instantly

The challenge fee goes to the echo's keeper inside the same transaction. No middleman, no payout queue.

No off switch

The game is a page that reads the chain. Anyone can host a copy. If we vanish, your hero and the arena live on.

07 · Questions

Before you raise.

Do I need crypto to play?

No. Practice runs fully in your browser for free. Play for real when you want your hero on chain.

What does it cost?

Raising a hero is about 25 cents. A challenge pays 2 cents to the echo's keeper, and a won forge about 1 cent. Network fees are under a cent.

Which wallets work?

Selene, Cashonize and Paytaca through WizardConnect. Duels use a small play satchel in your browser, so there are no popups mid fight. Send your hero home to your wallet any time.

Can someone cheat their way to a high level?

No. The contract replays every duel from both heroes' stats and the moves played. A level only exists if the win was real.

What happens if Evermore shuts down?

Nothing happens to your hero. It is a token in your wallet and the rules live in Bitcoin Cash contracts. Anyone can run the game page.
